Enterprise Lifecycle & Custom Hub Engineering

Global Nurture Program & Global Nurture Hub

$0 → $150M Pipeline Impact

Architected a global Marketo nurture program from scratch—growing from two tracks to multiple streams across 5–6 marketing teams, 10 languages, weekly interest-profile refreshes, and Velocity-driven promotional layers—while building a custom web app to centralize dual-quarter production.

The Challenge

Starting from zero and scaling rapidly across 5–6 marketing teams, multiple streams per track, 4–5 emails per track, 10 language versions, and dynamic promotional overlays created immense operational complexity. Managing two quarters at once—launching current while prepping next—in spreadsheets and Google Docs became unsustainable.

The Architecture & Partnership

Built the Marketo lifecycle engine from the ground up with a weekly-refreshing lead interest profile and Velocity tokens for modular promotional content. Partnered across content, promotional content, leadership, and activation/architecture teams, and engineered the Global Nurture Hub web app to centralize tracking, approvals, and execution.

The Outcome

Scaled from $0 to $150M in attributed pipeline impact, unified multi-team workflows across overlapping quarters, and gave global stakeholders a single source of truth as volume expanded.

Technical Debt Refactoring

84-Step Legacy Workflow Re-Architecture

Zero-Downtime System Overhaul

Audited, untangled, and rebuilt a brittle, 84-step operational processing workflow into a clean, modular, and self-documenting system.

The Challenge

Years of reactive patches had created an 84-step daisy-chained workflow prone to race conditions, silent sync failures, and routing delays that nobody on the team could safely modify.

The Architecture & Partnership

Mapped every dependency, webhook call, and Salesforce field update end-to-end. Collaborated with sales ops and engineering to consolidate redundant triggers, enforce strict order of operations, and build error-logging alerts.

The Outcome

Dramatically reduced processing latency and sync errors, restored stakeholder trust in lead routing, and created clear architectural documentation so future admins could scale safely.

Custom Internal Tooling & Code

Live Email Preview & Automated QA Web Apps

Custom HTML/JS & Apps Script

Built custom internal web applications to replace tedious manual QA checklists and fragmented stakeholder review cycles with instant, automated validation.

The Challenge

High-volume global email production relied on manual link-checking, UTM verification, and static screenshots—creating bottlenecks between copywriters, localization teams, and ops specialists.

The Architecture & Partnership

Developed bespoke lightweight web applications using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Google Apps Script that rendered live multi-variant previews and automatically audited tracking parameters, broken links, and token syntax.

The Outcome

Eliminated repetitive manual QA hours per campaign, caught formatting and tracking defects before deployment, and gave non-technical stakeholders a self-serve preview experience.

Cross-Functional Systems & Data Governance

Enterprise CRM Sync & API Integration Governance

Marketo ↔ SFDC ↔ Webhooks

Bridged the gap between marketing automation, Salesforce CRM, and external product/web endpoints to keep customer data accurate, compliant, and actionable.

The Challenge

Mismatched field schemas, API sync backlogs, and unstandardized webhook payloads caused friction between Marketing Operations, Sales Operations, and IT teams.

The Architecture & Partnership

Established clear data contracts across teams, optimized Marketo-SFDC sync filters, configured REST API/webhook enrichments, and enabled Marketo Sales Insight so reps received timely intent signals.

The Outcome

Improved sync throughput, strengthened data hygiene across systems, and gave sales and marketing leadership unified visibility from first touch to closed revenue.
