Enterprise Lifecycle & Global Scale

Global Nurture Hub Architecture

$80M → $150M Pipeline Impact

Designed and governed a centralized, multi-region nurture architecture supporting global enterprise teams at Google, enabling localized personalization without duplicating technical debt.

The Challenge

Regional marketing teams were operating in fragmented silos with inconsistent engagement logic, heavy manual campaign builds, and limited visibility into how nurture touches influenced enterprise pipeline.

The Architecture & Partnership

Partnered across regional field marketing, content, and analytics teams to architect a modular Global Nurture Hub in Marketo Engage. Standardized entry/exit governance, dynamic localization tokens, and CRM campaign attribution.

The Outcome

Generated $80M in attributed pipeline in its inaugural quarter and scaled to $150M across three subsequent quarters—while cutting regional campaign launch times and eliminating redundant build overhead.

Technical Debt Refactoring

84-Step Legacy Workflow Re-Architecture

Zero-Downtime System Overhaul

Audited, untangled, and rebuilt a brittle, 84-step operational processing workflow into a clean, modular, and self-documenting system.

The Challenge

Years of reactive patches had created an 84-step daisy-chained workflow prone to race conditions, silent sync failures, and routing delays that nobody on the team could safely modify.

The Architecture & Partnership

Mapped every dependency, webhook call, and Salesforce field update end-to-end. Collaborated with sales ops and engineering to consolidate redundant triggers, enforce strict order of operations, and build error-logging alerts.

The Outcome

Dramatically reduced processing latency and sync errors, restored stakeholder trust in lead routing, and created clear architectural documentation so future admins could scale safely.

Custom Internal Tooling & Code

Live Email Preview & Automated QA Web Apps

Custom HTML/JS & Apps Script

Built custom internal web applications to replace tedious manual QA checklists and fragmented stakeholder review cycles with instant, automated validation.

The Challenge

High-volume global email production relied on manual link-checking, UTM verification, and static screenshots—creating bottlenecks between copywriters, localization teams, and ops specialists.

The Architecture & Partnership

Developed bespoke lightweight web applications using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Google Apps Script that rendered live multi-variant previews and automatically audited tracking parameters, broken links, and token syntax.

The Outcome

Eliminated repetitive manual QA hours per campaign, caught formatting and tracking defects before deployment, and gave non-technical stakeholders a self-serve preview experience.
